Pies & Relish Plan

Four pies (apple, pumpkin, and two pecan) and a cranberry relish. The relish and apple filling get made Tuesday night, all four pies bake Wednesday, and Thursday is just whipping cream, scooping ice cream, and slicing.

Tuesday – Wednesday

Checkboxes save in this browser, so you can tick things off as you go.

Why bake everything Wednesday: the apple and pecan pies are both written to be baked up to a day ahead and kept at room temperature. Pumpkin is a custard, so it goes in the fridge once it cools. That frees the oven on Thursday for whoever is cooking dinner.

Tuesday, Nov 24 · evening

~1 hr
  • Do the shop
    Everything on the list below. Let the eggs and butter for the pecan pies come to room temperature on Wednesday.
  • Make the cranberry-orange relish (2×)
    5 min. Pulse it in the food processor in two batches until it's very finely chopped but still crunchy. Refrigerate. The recipe says it tastes better after a day or two.
  • Cook the apple filling
    25–30 min. Peel and slice the apples, then sauté in butter with the sugars, spices, and salt until soft but still holding their shape (Steps 1–4). Spread on a sheet pan to cool, then refrigerate. The recipe allows up to 24 hours ahead.
  • Crusts: keep the shells frozen
    Four shells stay frozen in their foil pans as the bottoms. Move the other four to the fridge before bed so they soften for the lattice tops. On Wednesday, ease each one out of its pan, press or roll it flat, and cut it into strips while the oven heats.

Wednesday, Nov 25

Bake day · ~4 hrs

One oven, in this order; the two pecan pies bake together at the end. Keep a rimmed sheet pan on the middle rack the whole time; every pie bakes on it.

  1. 0:00
    Preheat to 425°F with the sheet pan inside 425°
  2. 0:15
    Blind-bake the pumpkin crust 425° · ~20 min
    Line the frozen crust with foil, fill with pie weights or dried beans, and bake 15 min. Lift out the foil and weights and bake about 5 min more, until pale golden. Cool on a rack. If the shell package gives different blind-baking directions, follow those.
  3. 0:35
    Apple pie goes in 425° · 20 min
    Spread the cold filling in the frozen bottom crust, weave a lattice over the top, and press the strip ends into the edge. Brush the lattice with beaten egg and sprinkle with sugar. The gaps in the lattice do the job of steam vents.
  4. 0:55
    Turn the oven down to 375°F and bake 30–40 min more 375°
    It's done when the filling bubbles through the vents and the crust is golden brown.
  5. 1:30
    Apple pie out. Turn the oven up to 450°F 450°
    Cool on a rack at room temperature. Don't refrigerate it. While the oven heats, whisk the pumpkin filling together.
  6. 1:40
    Pumpkin pie goes in 450° · 10 min
    Pour the filling into the blind-baked crust, then lay the lattice strips gently on top (they'll float on the custard) and brush with egg. Any extra filling can bake in a small dish as crustless custard.
  7. 1:50
    Turn the oven down to 350°F and bake about 35 min more, until set 350°
    Meanwhile, start the pecan filling (one big batch for both pies): brown the butter, cook in the maple syrup, whisk in the honey, and let it cool 10 min. Then whisk it into the eggs, sugar, vanilla, and salt.
  8. 2:25
    Pumpkin pie out. Turn the oven up to 400°F 400°
    Cool it on a rack, then refrigerate once it's at room temperature.
  9. 2:35
    Both pecan pies go in 400° · 10 min
    Split the pecans between the two frozen crusts, divide the filling evenly over them, then lay a lattice on each and brush with egg. Two 9-inch pies usually fit side by side on a standard sheet pan; if not, use two pans on the same rack.
  10. 2:45
    Turn the oven down to 350°F and bake 35–45 min more 350°
    They're done when the centers have puffed up and turned golden brown. With two pies in, expect the longer end of the range.
  11. 3:25
    Pecan pies out. Sprinkle with flaky salt if you like.
    Cool at least 2 hours, then keep them covered at room temperature overnight. Oven off. Done.

Times are from when you turn the oven on. Pies vary, so check them at the low end of each range.

Thursday, Nov 26

No oven needed.

  • Set out the relish with dinner
    Give it a stir. Leftovers are good with yogurt or on a turkey sandwich.
  • Take the pumpkin pie out of the fridge
    About 30 min before dessert, so it isn't ice-cold.
  • Whip the cream right before dessert
    1 pint heavy cream with 2 Tbsp sugar, whipped to soft peaks. Keep the can of whipped cream as backup.
  • Take the ice cream out to soften
    About 10 min before serving so it scoops easily.
  • Slice the pies
    8 slices each, so 32 slices for 14 people. Plenty of people will want a sliver of two kinds, and there will be leftovers.
