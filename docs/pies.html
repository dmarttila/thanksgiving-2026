Pies & Relish Plan

Three pies and a cranberry relish. The relish and apple filling get made Tuesday night, all three pies bake Wednesday, and Thursday is just whipping cream, scooping ice cream, and slicing.

Tuesday – Wednesday

Checkboxes save in this browser, so you can tick things off as you go.

Why bake everything Wednesday: the apple and pecan pies are both written to be baked up to a day ahead and kept at room temperature. Pumpkin is a custard, so it goes in the fridge once it cools. That frees the oven on Thursday for whoever is cooking dinner.

Tuesday, Nov 24 · evening

~1 hr
  • Do the shop
    Everything on the list below. Let the eggs and butter for the pecan pie come to room temperature on Wednesday.
  • Make the cranberry-orange relish (2×)
    5 min. Pulse it in the food processor in two batches until it's very finely chopped but still crunchy. Refrigerate. The recipe says it tastes better after a day or two.
  • Cook the apple filling
    25–30 min. Peel and slice the apples, then sauté in butter with the sugars, spices, and salt until soft but still holding their shape (Steps 1–4). Spread on a sheet pan to cool, then refrigerate. The recipe allows up to 24 hours ahead.
  • Line the three pie plates and freeze them
    Unroll one store-bought crust into each pie plate, crimp the edges, and freeze. The recipes allow up to 24 hours. Keep the fourth crust (the apple pie's top) in the fridge.

Wednesday, Nov 25

Bake day · ~4 hrs

One oven, one pie at a time, in this order. Keep a rimmed sheet pan on the middle rack the whole time; every pie bakes on it.

  1. 0:00
    Preheat to 425°F with the sheet pan inside 425°
  2. 0:15
    Blind-bake the pumpkin crust 425° · ~20 min
    Line the frozen crust with foil, fill with pie weights or dried beans, and bake 15 min. Lift out the foil and weights and bake about 5 min more, until pale golden. Cool on a rack. If the crust box gives different blind-baking directions, follow those.
  3. 0:35
    Apple pie goes in 425° · 20 min
    Spread the cold filling in the frozen bottom crust, unroll the fourth crust over the top, crimp, brush with cream or egg, sprinkle with sugar, and cut steam vents.
  4. 0:55
    Turn the oven down to 375°F and bake 30–40 min more 375°
    It's done when the filling bubbles through the vents and the crust is golden brown.
  5. 1:30
    Apple pie out. Turn the oven up to 450°F 450°
    Cool on a rack at room temperature. Don't refrigerate it. While the oven heats, whisk the pumpkin filling together.
  6. 1:40
    Pumpkin pie goes in 450° · 10 min
    Pour the filling into the blind-baked crust. Any extra can bake in a small dish as crustless custard.
  7. 1:50
    Turn the oven down to 350°F and bake about 35 min more, until set 350°
    Meanwhile, start the pecan filling: brown the butter, cook in the maple syrup, whisk in the honey, and let it cool 10 min. Then whisk it into the eggs, sugars, vanilla, salt, and bourbon.
  8. 2:25
    Pumpkin pie out. Turn the oven up to 400°F 400°
    Cool it on a rack, then refrigerate once it's at room temperature.
  9. 2:35
    Pecan pie goes in 400° · 10 min
    Spread the pecans over the frozen crust and pour the filling over them.
  10. 2:45
    Turn the oven down to 350°F and bake 35–45 min more 350°
    It's done when the center has puffed up and turned golden brown.
  11. 3:25
    Pecan pie out. Sprinkle with flaky salt if you like.
    Cool at least 2 hours, then keep it covered at room temperature overnight. Oven off. Done.

Times are from when you turn the oven on. Pies vary, so check them at the low end of each range.

Thursday, Nov 26

No oven needed.

  • Set out the relish with dinner
    Give it a stir. Leftovers are good with yogurt or on a turkey sandwich.
  • Take the pumpkin pie out of the fridge
    About 30 min before dessert, so it isn't ice-cold.
  • Whip the cream right before dessert
    1 pint heavy cream with 2 Tbsp sugar, whipped to soft peaks. Keep the can of whipped cream as backup.
  • Take the ice cream out to soften
    About 10 min before serving so it scoops easily.
  • Slice the pies
    8 slices each, so 24 slices for 14 people. Plenty of people will want a sliver of two kinds.

Recipes

Classic Apple Pie ↗

Melissa Clark
1× · 8 slices
  • 3½ lb apples, peeled, cored, in ¼-inch wedges (~11 cups)
  • 2 Tbsp unsalted butter
  • ½ cup light brown sugar · 2 Tbsp granulated sugar
  • 1 tsp cinnamon · ½ tsp ginger · ½ tsp nutmeg · pinch of cloves
  • ¼ tsp fine sea salt · 2 Tbsp cornstarch
  • 1½ tsp lemon juice · ½ tsp lemon zest
  • 2 store-bought crusts (bottom and top)
  • Cream or beaten egg, demerara sugar (optional)

When: Filling Tuesday night. Bake Wednesday: 20 min at 425°F, then 30–40 min at 375°F. Keep at room temperature.

Maple-Honey Pecan Pie ↗

Melissa Clark
1× · 8 slices
  • 1 store-bought crust
  • ½ cup (1 stick) unsalted butter
  • ¼ cup maple syrup · ¼ cup honey
  • ½ cup light brown sugar
  • ½ cup maple sugar (or more brown sugar)
  • 3 large eggs, room temperature
  • 1 Tbsp bourbon (optional) · 1 tsp vanilla
  • ¾ tsp kosher salt · 1½ cups pecan halves
  • Flaky sea salt (optional)

When: Wednesday, last in the oven: 10 min at 400°F, then 35–45 min at 350°F. Keeps 24+ hrs at room temperature.

Pumpkin Pie ↗

The New York Times
1× · one 9-inch pie
  • 2 cups pumpkin purée
  • 3 eggs
  • 2 Tbsp granulated sugar · 2 Tbsp brown sugar
  • 2 Tbsp molasses
  • 1½ cups heavy cream
  • ½ tsp ground cloves · 2 tsp cinnamon
  • 2 Tbsp grated fresh ginger (or 1 tsp ground)
  • ½ tsp salt
  • 1 store-bought crust, blind-baked

When: Wednesday: whisk everything, pour into the crust, 10 min at 450°F, then ~35 min at 350°F. Refrigerate once cool.

Cranberry-Orange Relish ↗

Martha Rose Shulman
2× · 16 servings
  • 2 bags fresh cranberries
  • 2 navel oranges, skin on, in chunks
  • 1 cup pecans
  • ⅔ cup mild honey

When: Tuesday night. Process in two batches; it must be a food processor, not a blender.

Equipment check

Find these before Tuesday.

Metal pie plates, if you can. Both Melissa Clark recipes call for metal. Glass doesn't conduct heat as well, so the bottom crust may not cook through. With glass, blind-bake the pecan crust the same way as the pumpkin one (15 min with weights, then 5 min uncovered), and consider doing the same for the apple pie's bottom crust.

  • Three 9-inch pie plates, metal preferred
  • Rimmed sheet pan for the oven (all pies bake on it)
  • A second sheet pan for cooling the apple filling
  • Large skillet (apple filling)
  • Small saucepan (browned butter and syrup)
  • Food processor (relish)
  • Pie weights or dried beans
  • Wire cooling rack
  • Mixing bowls, whisk, rubber spatula
  • Pastry brush
  • Fridge space for the relish, apple filling, and pumpkin pie
